About

I recently completed a Master of Information Technology in Artificial Intelligence at the University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney), after a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering and Automation at Beijing Institute of Technology, Zhuhai.

My research asks a simple question: when an AI system scores well, what does that score actually tell us? I work on the evaluation validity of AI systems across three settings:

  • LLMs in education: agreement between automated and expert scoring, and how LLM-generated formative feedback is selected and structured.
  • Robustness of NLP models: human-validated, stratified diagnostics of where sentiment models fail.
  • ML for power systems: methodological scrutiny of benchmarks for power-system attack detection.

I am looking for Ph.D. and research assistant opportunities. Feel free to reach out.
